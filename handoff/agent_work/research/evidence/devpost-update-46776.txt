ForgeHacks is LIVE: track prompts and your participant packet
Hello builders,
ForgeHacks 2026 is LIVE. The build window is open until Saturday, October 10 at 12:00 PM ET.
 🧠 Choose your track 
ForgeHacks has six tracks:
🏥 AI + Healthcare
📚 AI + Education
🌎 AI + Climate
💼 AI + Business
🤖 AI + Cybersecurity
🎨 AI + Creativity
Your track must be selected when you submit your project and cannot be changed afterward.
YOUR PARTICIPANT PACKET
https://drive.google.com/file/d/10DcagvYeoBv7NEyu33H_6ciDRE5UMOsW/view?usp=sharing
Everything you need is in it: the six track prompts, your sponsor credits and how to redeem them, the schedule, the rules, how to get mentor help, and the submission checklist.
WHAT TO DO NOW
1. Open the packet and read the six track prompts. Pick one track and build around its prompt.
2. Join the Discord: https://discord.gg/s5U4xUWXc
Announcements, team finding, mentor help and the workshop schedule all live there.
3. Form your team. You can build solo or with up to 4 people, and every member must be registered on Devpost.
4. Redeem the sponsor credits you plan to use. Every participant gets up to $785 in credits. The codes and steps are in the packet.
5. Start building. Your project has to be built during the event. Existing libraries, frameworks and boilerplate are fine.
KEY DATES (all times ET)
Workshops: October 4 to 9. The schedule is pinned in Discord and every session is recorded.
Submissions lock: Saturday, October 10 at 12:00 PM ET. Submit at https://forgehacks-2026.devpost.com/
Judging and public voting: October 10 to 11
Winners announced: Monday, October 12 at 3:00 PM ET
NEED HELP?
Open a ticket in #mentor-help on Discord any time before submissions lock, and a mentor will pick it up. For anything else, email [email protected].
One correction: the Featherless AI credit is $25 in request credits, not unlimited access as we announced earlier. The setup steps are in the packet.
Good luck. Go build something.

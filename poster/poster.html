UNIT V: BASICS OF INVESTMENT

1. Investment Return

The gain on an investment, as a % of money invested. Return = (Gain ÷ Invested) × 100
Eg: Invest ₹10,000, get back ₹11,000.
Return = 1,000 ÷ 10,000 = 10%

2. Compounding Frequency

More times interest is added per year = more money. A = P (1 + r/m)mt
Eg: ₹10,000 at 12% for 1 year

3. Uneven Cash Flows

Money received is different each year. Bring each back to today. PV = CF1/(1+i) + CF2/(1+i)2
Eg: ₹1,000 (yr 1) and ₹2,000 (yr 2), i = 10%
PV = 909 + 1,653 = ₹2,562

4. Economic Equivalence

Different amounts at different times can be worth the same. F = P (1 + i)n
Eg: at 10%, ₹100 today = ₹110 after 1 year
UNIT VI: RISK AND UNCERTAINTY

1. Decision under Risk

Risk = chances known. Pick the best average (EMV). EMV = Σ (chance × payoff)
50%: ₹100, 50%: ₹40 ⇒ EMV = ₹70

2. Decision under Uncertainty

Chances unknown. Use a rule:
  • Maximax: best of the best
  • Maximin: best of the worst
Eg: Stock (80 or −20), Bond (40 or 20)
Maximax → Stock
Maximin → Bond

3. Risk Premium

Extra return for taking risk. Premium = Expected − Safe rate
Eg: 12% − 6% = 6%

4. Portfolio Diversification

Don't put all eggs in one basket: more different assets = less risk.

5. Life Insurance

You pay a premium; the company pays the sum assured if you die. Premium ≈ Sum assured × P(death)
Eg: ₹1,00,000 × 0.01 = ₹1,000

6. Endowment

Pays if you die in the term OR survive to the end. Endowment = Insurance + Savings
Higher risk ⇒ higher expected return
